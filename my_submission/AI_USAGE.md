# AI Usage Disclosure

AI assistance was used throughout this assignment for reasoning support,
experiment design, calculation checking, code explanations, and documentation.

## Where AI helped

AI assistance was used to:

- Understand and break the assignment into smaller experiments.
- Explain Python, tokenization, Unicode normalization, and measurement concepts.
- Suggest controlled experiments and sanity checks.
- Review calculations and compare alternative measurement definitions.
- Help identify potential implementation and methodological issues.
- Help structure the Part A and Part B documentation after measurements
  were performed.
- Help structure the Part C decision analysis and recommendation memo.

## Verification of evidence

All experimental numbers reported in the submission were obtained from:

- the supplied starter resources,
- the locally created evaluation corpus,
- tokenizer runs performed locally,
- calculations performed by local scripts, or
- the supplied benchmark log and model specification.

AI-generated numbers were not accepted as experimental evidence without
local verification.

## Where AI initially misled the analysis

At one point, AI suggested a reviewer-throughput figure before confirming
that the assignment actually specified a review rate.

This was corrected.

The assignment specifies 10 reviewer-hours per week but does not specify
examples reviewed per hour. Therefore, the final submission does not treat
reviewer throughput as a supplied fact.

For Part C, a 2-minute/example rate is used only as an explicitly labeled
planning assumption when calculating a possible review capacity.

## Part C evidence limitation

The supplied resources do not contain an executable Part C model,
baseline outputs, a <=1B rewriter checkpoint, or SFT benchmark results.

Therefore, no numerical Part C quality improvement, latency improvement,
training time, or dollar cost is claimed as measured evidence.

The Part C recommendation is a decision under the stated constraints,
supported by explicitly labeled assumptions and a proposed Day-1 experiment.

## Final responsibility

AI assistance was used to support reasoning and documentation, but the
experimental evidence was run or verified locally and the final submission
was reviewed by me.
